TierPoint: Paving the Way for Secure and Reliable IT Solutions

In today's digital age, technology has become an indispensable aspect of business operations. As companies increasingly rely on IT services and solutions to power their operations, finding the right partner becomes crucial. TierPoint stands out in this landscape with its comprehensive suite of managed cloud services, private cloud solutions, disaster recovery options, and robust security framework. This article explores how TierPoint helps organizations of all sizes meet their IT infrastructure needs while maintaining the highest standards of security and performance.

Managed Cloud Services

TierPoint's managed cloud services support both public and private cloud deployments, offering solutions for multitenant and enterprise-level environments. The company manages infrastructure for major platforms including Microsoft Azure and Amazon Web Services, providing scalable infrastructure solutions that can be deployed across multiple US data centers.

Cloud connectivity is a key component of TierPoint's service offering, with secure connections established between customer networks and public cloud providers through dedicated managed network services. Data centers feature robust security measures, with 24x7x365 electronic and on-site security operations supported by two-factor authentication including biometric systems and 90-day retention CCTV surveillance.

The company's IT advisory services complement its cloud management capabilities, offering strategic guidance across several areas including hybrid cloud architecture, security assessments, and application modernization. Through its DevOps service offerings, TierPoint helps organizations accelerate software development cycles while maintaining high standards of quality and security.

Private Cloud Solutions

The company's private cloud solution allows customers to maintain complete control over their infrastructure, data, and applications. This architecture enables the isolation required for compliance-sensitive environments while delivering the performance and uptime standards expected in enterprise-level deployments.

TierPoint's private cloud capabilities support multiple deployment options, including dedicated pods within their US data centers and customized solutions for specific customer requirements. Security is a core component of the service offering, with 24x7x365 electronic and on-site security operations supported by biometric systems and 90-day retention CCTV surveillance. This robust security framework ensures secure data storage and transmission while maintaining the performance expected in enterprise environments.

The company's managed services team provides ongoing support for private cloud operations, including database management and help desk services. Availability options range from traditional on-premises configurations to hybrid cloud deployments that allow organizations to maintain full control over sensitive data while leveraging public cloud capabilities for application hosting or disaster recovery purposes. This flexibility enables customers to tailor their cloud infrastructure to specific business requirements while maintaining the security and control needed for mission-critical applications.

Disaster Recovery and IT Advisory Services

TierPoint delivers comprehensive disaster recovery solutions across multiple frameworks, including Disaster Recovery as a Service (DRaaS) and Backup as a Service (BaaS). The company's DRaaS platform offers cloud-based recovery solutions with recovery times measured in minutes, supporting major frameworks including Zerto, Nutanix Leap, and vCloud Director Availability. For broader protection, BaaS services employ enterprise-grade backup capabilities powered by Commvault, ensuring robust data availability across both cloud and on-premises environments.

The managed services team supports these solutions with continuous monitoring and maintenance. Database management services maintain high availability, data protection, and performance for MySQL and SQL databases, encompassing full monitoring, configuration, and technical support. Help desk services provide comprehensive U.S.-based technical support, available 24x7 across multiple tiers to ensure rapid issue resolution.

TierPoint's approach to security complements its disaster recovery services through a multi-layered security framework. The company employs comprehensive next-generation firewall solutions with proactive management capabilities, unified security incident detection platforms, and multi-factor authentication services. These security solutions are further enhanced by robust antivirus services and managed Web Application Firewall capabilities designed to protect against DDoS attacks.

To support business continuity, TierPoint offers a suite of architectural and consulting services. The company's IT advisory consultants help organizations balance cost-effectiveness with performance and security standards through a cloud-agnostic approach. They specialize in hybrid cloud consulting, comprehensive IT security assessments, business continuity planning, and application modernization strategies that leverage modern architectural frameworks including containers, microservices, and serverless architectures.

Security Solutions

TierPoint's security framework relies on several key components to protect both cloud and on-premises environments. The company employs next-generation firewall solutions through its CleanIP NGFW service, which incorporates proactive management capabilities to detect and mitigate threats in real-time.

For comprehensive security monitoring, TierPoint's CleanIP XDR platform unifies various security sources into a single operational environment. This tool enables security teams to detect, investigate, and respond to incidents more effectively, combining threat intelligence with automated workflows.

In addition to these technical solutions, TierPoint offers managed multi-factor authentication (MMFA) through its CleanIP MMFA service. This service provides an extra layer of security for login attempts, requiring users to verify their identity through multiple methods before gaining access to systems or data.

The company's security portfolio also includes managed antivirus services and a managed Web Application Firewall solution to protect against Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks. Together, these tools form a robust security foundation that tiers between cloud environments, protecting data centers that are SOC 1 Type II, SOC 2 Type II, and HIPAA compliant.

Support and Customer Success

TierPoint's comprehensive support services are designed to deliver 24x7 technical assistance, with U.S.-based professionals available across multiple service tiers. The company's database management services maintain high availability, data protection, and performance for MySQL and SQL databases, encompassing full monitoring, configuration, and technical support.

The managed services team supports TierPoint's diverse customer base, which includes healthcare providers, government agencies, and insurance companies. Their expertise ranges from IT disaster recovery and security consulting to application modernization and hybrid cloud architecture. The company's service delivery team, including operations technicians and service delivery consultants, works closely with customers to ensure the reliability of TierPoint's critical infrastructure.

TierPoint's customer success approach emphasizes collaboration and employee input. The company values its workforce's ideas and contributions, fostering a supportive environment that helps employees develop their careers. This focus extends to all departments, from technical support to human resources, where the company maintains a family-first mentality and flexible work arrangements.
