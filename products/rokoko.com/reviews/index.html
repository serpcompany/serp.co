Rokoko Revolutionizes Full-Body Motion Capture with Accurate, Accessible Technology

Rokoko has established itself as a leading innovator in full-body motion capture technology, offering solutions that blend precision with accessibility for creators of all sizes. Their suite of tools, from the Smartsuit Pro to Rokoko Vision, represents significant advancements in tracking accuracy while maintaining the flexibility needed for both professional studios and independent projects. In this article, we'll explore how Rokoko's technology works, its advantages over traditional methods, and the company's commitment to making high-quality motion capture more accessible to artists worldwide.

Rokoko's Capture Tools

Rokoko offers a comprehensive suite of motion capture tools designed for both professional studios and indie creators. Their flagship product, the Smartsuit Pro II, captures full-body motion through its 9DOF sensor technology, achieving remarkable 3D orientation accuracy of ±1 degree. This precise tracking solution includes finger tracking via the Smartgloves, which plug directly into the suit for seamless hand and wrist motion capture.

The company's third-generation tracking technology, built on Sensor Fusion 2.0, represents a significant advancement in motion capture accuracy. This enhanced system delivers a 24% improvement over previous methodologies when compared against high-end optical mocap standards. The technology demonstrates particularly strong performance in challenging environments with metal or magnetic interference, ensuring consistent capture quality even in demanding conditions.

While the Smartsuit Pro II delivers exceptional tracking capabilities, Rokoko recognizes the importance of additional focused tracking for specific applications. The Coil Pro solution addresses this need by providing pinpoint capture precision for both the Smartsuit Pro II and Smartgloves using EMF motion capture technology. This approach eliminates common issues like drift and occlusion, offering real-time global positioning for both body and finger movements.

To further address the needs of facial performers, Rokoko developed the Headcam and Headrig systems. These lightweight devices work in concert with the company's existing suits, offering the most extensive capture range at just 240 grams per unit. Together, these components enable full-body animation experiences without the need for additional cameras or markers, operating within a 100m wireless tracking range that requires only standard WiFi infrastructure.

The company's ecosystem extends beyond hardware with the introduction of Rokoko Vision, a free AI-based motion capture tool that dramatically reduces entry barriers for new users. This software solution supports both single- and dual-camera setups, with the latter configuration delivering significant improvements in tracking accuracy through increased input. Users can capture their movements through standard videos and quickly convert them into 3D animations, with the system supporting recordings up to 15 seconds in length for free usage.

Rokoko's commitment to accessibility is further evidenced by their discounted Indie Bundle offering, which combines multiple capture tools and software solutions at a 40% savings. This inclusive approach has garnered praise from industry experts, including a world-renowned digital artist who has thoroughly reviewed both the Smartsuit Pro II and Smartgloves. The company stands behind its products with a generous 30-day trial period that includes a full refund guarantee, with customers receiving detailed tracking information throughout the shipping process.

Software and Integration

Rokoko Studio serves as the core software solution for character animation, supporting real-time mocap data manipulation through retargeting, custom character import, and pose correction. The software enables users to filter capture data with options for root locking, foot locking changes, elevation tracking, and knee pop smoothing, while allowing export in both .FBX and .BVH formats with industry-compatible skeleton presets including Human IK and Mixamo.

Through native integrations, Rokoko Studio supports real-time mocap transmission to multiple 3D software platforms including Unreal Engine, Unity, Blender, Maya, Cinema 4D, Houdini, MotionBuilder, and iClone, with the ability to capture data from the company's full suite of devices including the Smartsuit Pro II, Smartgloves, Face Capture app, Headcam, and Headrig. The software also includes mesh export functionality and custom export overrides to accommodate specific project requirements.

The company's motion capture system demonstrates significant advantages over video-based approaches, particularly in terms of tracking flexibility and environmental resilience. While A.I. motion capture tools typically struggle with performers outside the camera frame or complex body positions, Rokoko's dual-camera setup delivers improved tracking accuracy. The inertial mocap technology enables simultaneous capture of up to five performers and operates effectively within a 100m WiFi range, demonstrating superior performance in challenging environments compared to traditional video-based solutions.

Indie Creator Program

This dedicated section further elaborates on Rokoko's comprehensive support for independent creators, highlighting the company's commitment to making professional motion capture technology accessible to small studios and individual artists.

Rokoko has developed an Indie Creator Program offering three bundle configurations, each priced at a 40% discount compared to standard retail prices (see [doc 1]). The program meets specific eligibility criteria, with independent creators and small studios needing to demonstrate annual revenues below $100,000 USD and have no more than three full-time employees (see [doc 1]).

The company presents multiple purchase options, including a custom bundle configuration available upon request through sales@rokoko.com (see [doc 2]). This flexible approach allows creators to tailor their setup based on specific needs, with the most comprehensive bundle including the full Performance Capture package (see [doc 1]).

Once qualified, customers receive a simple order process that allows placement once eligibility is confirmed (see [doc 1]). All bundled solutions ship with a 30-day trial guarantee, allowing creators to test the technology thoroughly before committing (see [doc 1]).

Beyond hardware, Rokoko's software ecosystem offers substantial value to indie creators through its freemium model. The company's Rokoko Studio software is available free of charge for basic features including mocap recording and export (see [doc 3]). Advanced capabilities, such as real-time streaming to external 3D software, require a Plus or Pro subscription tier (see [doc 3]).

The company's monthly installment payment option through Splitit offers significant flexibility, allowing USA customers to spread payments over multiple months (see [doc 1]). To facilitate adoption, Rokoko provides free personal Zoom demonstrations of their mocap tools, with a dedicated team including Francesco and Paulina hosting these sessions from their Copenhagen office (see [doc 1]).

This comprehensive support structure builds upon Rokoko's existing suite of capture tools and software solutions, which have garnered praise for their real-time character animation capabilities across multiple 3D software platforms (see [doc 4]). The company's focus on accessibility extends through detailed support resources including video guides, a Help Center, and dedicated support engineers, creating a supportive environment for creators to master the technology (see [doc 1]).

Battery and Usage Requirements

Users must purchase their own power supplies due to customs restrictions, with specific recommendations for battery requirements. The Smartsuit Pro II and Smartgloves require powerbanks or external batteries due to international shipping regulations.

The company provides detailed recommendations for compatible batteries, including specific model numbers and capacities. These guidelines help ensure optimal performance across different tracking scenarios and environmental conditions.

Battery requirements vary by device:

  • Smartsuit Pro II operates on 4 AAA batteries per sensor hub

  • Smartgloves require a single AAA battery

Rokoko offers a 30-day trial guarantee, during which customers can test the equipment thoroughly before committing. The company provides clear instructions for setting up and managing power sources to maintain consistent performance throughout the trial period.

VisionAI and Dual-Camera Functionality

Rokoko's free AI motion capture tool, Rokoko Vision, supports both single- and dual-camera setups. The system processes video input from either a webcam or uploaded footage, enabling users to create 3D animations in minutes after account creation and login.

The tool distinguishes itself through dual-camera functionality that significantly enhances motion reconstruction accuracy compared to single-camera input. By using two cameras, Rokoko Vision improves depth estimation and provides clearer views of performers' bodies, effectively reducing occlusion effects that commonly plague single-camera systems. This multi-camera approach represents a critical improvement over video-based motion capture techniques, which often struggle with performers positioned outside the camera frame or exhibiting complex body movements.

For optimal performance, users should ensure the following technical requirements:

  • Log in using Rokoko Studio (not Studio Legacy)

  • Use a stable internet connection

  • Verify account settings and team selection in Rokoko Studio

  • Restart the software to refresh data

After completing these setup steps, users can begin capturing motion data through simple account creation and login. The process involves uploading video content, with the system typically requiring 30-40 seconds to process every 10 seconds of data. While processing time may vary based on server load, users should expect this standard processing timeline for their captured data.

The system imposes certain limitations on input requirements to ensure optimal performance:

  • Capture one full-body performer at a time

  • Avoid loose clothing, particularly dark or black garments

  • Position performers within a well-lit environment featuring a clear background

  • Ensure camera stability using a tripod or similar support

Users should maintain these conditions throughout the capture process to achieve the best possible results. For technical troubleshooting, Rokoko provides comprehensive documentation through their website, including detailed instructions for common resolution issues.

Rokoko recommends testing dual-camera functionality during a free 14-day trial period before subscribing to the Plus plan, which enables full access to this advanced feature. This period allows users to verify their system's configuration and performance before committing to a paid subscription.
