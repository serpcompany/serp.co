Painted Saintly

Transform Your Photos into Uniquely Styled Saintlike Portraits with AI Power

In an era where personalized artistry meets advanced technology, Painted Saintly stands as a pioneering force, transforming ordinary photos into extraordinary Saintlike portraits through cutting-edge AI. This innovation has garnered attention from tech-savvy users at prestigious institutions like Google and VCU, while its New York City presence serves thousands of satisfied customers. Through our detailed exploration of this digital artistry, we'll uncover how users turn their most cherished photos into unique masterpieces, while the company maintains rigorous privacy standards that protect both data and creativity.

Company Overview

The Painted Saintly Company specializes in transforming personal photos into uniquely styled Saintlike portraits using advanced AI technology. Since their launch, the company has created over 10,000 portraits for more than 1,000 satisfied customers, establishing a presence in New York City and gaining recognition among tech users from various institutions including Google, UMD, VCU, and Yahoo.

To begin a project, users create an account and follow a straightforward process: each portrait series requires 20 user-submitted photos, which can include various perspectives, backgrounds, facial expressions, and both close-ups of the face and full-body shots. The company recommends using high-resolution images taken in good lighting to achieve the best results. The entire process typically concludes within 30 minutes, though processing times may vary based on current demand.

The custom AI model generates 30 unique portraits per project, with each session costing $9.99. Additional portraits can be purchased in sets of 20 for $3.99 each. All generated portraits remain the intellectual property of the user, with the AI model and associated imagery immediately deleted from server logs after completion.

For those who wish to display their portrait creations, Painted Saintly offers flexible customization options including digital print downloads and physical product integration. Users can select their favorite portraits for printing on various items such as candles, canvases, or mugs before arranging shipment directly to their location. The complete process aims to maintain stringent data privacy standards, with uploaded photos stored securely on Amazon Web Services (AWS) and all customer data encrypted for protection.

How It Works

After creating a user account, customers initiate a project by giving it a name and uploading 20 images that showcase different perspectives, expressions, and environments. These photos should be high-quality and well-lit to ensure the AI produces the best possible results.

The custom AI model processes each project quickly, typically completing the generation of 30 unique Saintlike portraits within 30 minutes. The system supports multiple file formats including PNG, JPG, and HEIC, though it specifically recommends these formats for optimal performance.

Upon completion, users can view their portraits on the project page where they select their favorites for customization. The company offers several product options for displaying the portraits, including digital print downloads and physical items like candles, canvases, and mugs. All selected products are shipped directly to the customer's specified address, with each item displaying the individual portraits they've chosen.

Customization and Products

After the AI model generates 30 unique portraits, users can select their favorites for customization through various product options. The service supports digital print downloads as well as physical items including candles, canvases, and mugs. Each selected product displays the user's chosen portrait, with customization options allowing precise selection of preferred images.

Users have the flexibility to purchase additional portrait sets in batches of 20 for $3.99 each, giving them more options for their customized piece. The company's technical specifications prioritize high-quality input images, recommending high-resolution photos taken in good lighting for the best results. These technical requirements ensure that the AI model produces the most accurate and detailed Saintlike portraits possible.

To facilitate product selection and purchase, users follow these steps after completing their project:

  1. View their 30 generated portraits on the project page

  2. Select the desired image(s) for customization

  3. Choose their preferred product type (digital print or physical item)

  4. Use the provided portrait ID to add items to their cart on the Shopify store

  5. Complete the secure checkout process through Stripe

The company's commitment to data privacy is reflected in their storage practices, with all uploaded images encrypted and deleted from servers within 30 days of processing. This approach ensures that customer data remains secure while supporting the efficient delivery of personalized Saintlike portraits through their innovative AI service.

Technical Details

The AI model operates with specific technical requirements to produce the best possible results. Users must upload high-resolution images taken in good lighting, with supported file formats including PNG, JPG, and HEIC. Each portrait project requires 20 images showcasing various perspectives, backgrounds, facial expressions, and including both close-up face shots and full-body images to provide the necessary variation for AI processing.

The company's custom AI model generates 30 unique portraits per project, with the entire process typically concluding within 30 minutes. Amazon Web Services (AWS) securely stores all uploaded data, processing the images to create the custom Saintlike portraits using the user's provided photographs. Once the portraits are generated, both the custom AI model and the original images are permanently deleted from server logs, with the encryption ensuring data security throughout the process.

Customer data is protected through rigorous privacy practices, with all uploaded images deleted from servers within 30 days and no customer data ever being shared or sold. The payment processing system employs Stripe for secure transactions, a service used by companies including Amazon, Google, Lyft, and Shopify to handle financial information. Painted Saintly never accesses or stores customer financial data, maintaining strict privacy standards for all transactions and data handling procedures.

Privacy and Security

During the 30-day storage period, all uploaded images are securely encrypted and stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud infrastructure. This practice ensures that customer data remains protected and follows industry-standard security protocols. After the initial processing window, both the custom AI model files and original image data are permanently deleted from server logs, with no possibility of recovery. This comprehensive data management approach aligns with Painted Saintly's commitment to maintaining strict privacy standards throughout their operations.

Financial transactions and data handling utilize Stripe's secure processing methods, a platform trusted by major companies such as Amazon, Google, Lyft, and Shopify. When customers make payments through Painted Saintly, their financial information is handled directly by Stripe, with the company never possessing or storing this data. This secure arrangement provides an additional layer of protection for customer information throughout the transaction process.
