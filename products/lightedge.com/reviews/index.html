Lightedge: Building Secure, Sustainable IT Infrastructure for Today's Digital Economy

In today's digital age, businesses across industries rely heavily on information technology infrastructure to operate. For companies in highly regulated sectors, finding a dependable provider of enterprise-class IT services is crucial. Lightedge has positioned itself as a leader in this space through strategic acquisitions and a commitment to environmental sustainability. This article explores the company's leadership structure, environmental initiatives, and core services, including its managed AWS solutions and colocation offerings. Through these details, we uncover how Lightedge has become one of the nation's most secure and compliant infrastructure providers, serving over 1,700 customers in industries that demand the highest levels of data security and operational reliability.

Company Leadership and Culture

Lightedge has built a leadership team that brings a wealth of experience in enterprise IT, security, and innovation. Rob Carter, as CEO, sets the strategic direction, while Michael Hannan serves as both CIO and CISO, ensuring that Lightedge maintains its competitive edge in information security. Jody Gehl leads the human resources function, focusing on Diversity, Equality, and Inclusion (DEI) initiatives that foster a culture of innovation and continuous improvement.

The company's leadership structure includes Lisa McLin as Chief Sales Officer, Adam Williams as Chief Customer Officer, and Dale Levesque as Vice President of Product. This diverse team brings together sales and marketing expertise with deep technical knowledge, allowing Lightedge to deliver both innovative technical solutions and customer-centric service. Aileen Curtin's role as Director of the Cloud Center of Excellence demonstrates the company's commitment to staying at the forefront of cloud technology developments.

In terms of culture, Lightedge emphasizes environmental responsibility through sustainable infrastructure practices that optimize energy efficiency while reducing power usage, water consumption, and waste. The company's commitment to building stronger, healthier communities aligns with broader DEI initiatives that invite the best candidates to join the team and help them reach their full potential. Through these combined efforts, Lightedge aims to create a positive impact both in the technology industry and in local communities across the United States.

Environmental Responsibility and Infrastructure

Lightedge's sustainable infrastructure approach centers on optimizing energy efficiency while minimizing environmental impact through power usage, water consumption, and waste reduction. The company has developed robust compliance frameworks for enterprise data centers across the United States and key international markets, serving over 1300 clients in highly regulated industries via hybrid cloud, colocation, connectivity, and managed services.

The company's 18 strategic data centers across 12 U.S. markets provide a secure and compliant infrastructure platform for managing over 1700 customer workloads. This hybrid colocation offering combines Connectria's IBM and public cloud maturity with Lightedge's existing capabilities, creating one of the nation's most secure and compliant infrastructure providers for hybrid colocation and multi-cloud solutions.

In technical implementation, Lightedge colocation services offer a comprehensive suite of options including shared colo, cabinets, cages, and private suites. These facilities support high-density power requirements with scalable space options and energy-efficient cooling systems. The company's data centers maintain strict security protocols with biometric access controls, 24/7 monitoring, and compliance certifications including SOC 1/2/3, HIPAA, HITRUST, and ISO 27001.

The company's environmental stewardship extends to its approach to water consumption and waste reduction. While specific figures on water consumption efficiency are not provided in the available documents, Lightedge demonstrates a commitment to sustainable operations through its tiered data center design, which incorporates N+1 and 2N power/cooling redundancy systems to enhance reliability without unnecessary resource consumption.

Looking at the technical support infrastructure, Lightedge's 24/7/365 remote hands support is available from certified infrastructure engineers, with the option for 24/7/265 support in certain cases. The company's hybrid capabilities enable direct connections to AWS, Azure, and other major cloud providers, while maintaining compliance certifications and advanced security protocols across all facilities.

Managed AWS Solutions

Lightedge's managed AWS solutions offer 24/7 real-time monitoring across all AWS services with proactive issue detection and resolution. The company's advanced security management features include expertly managed IAM policies, automated patching, and continuous threat monitoring. Their ongoing cost optimization services provide managed resource rightsizing, waste reduction, and adherence to FinOps best practices.

For proactive infrastructure management, Lightedge handles updates, performs performance tuning, and automates backups. Their security and compliance framework features 24/7 threat detection and response capabilities. The infrastructure includes built-in controls, comprehensive reporting, and audit support while providing advanced encryption, identity validation, and integrity monitoring.

Lightedge's professional services team consists of AWS-certified security specialists who can help customers align their cloud strategy with business goals. Their service offerings include cloud strategy and readiness assessments, well-architected reviews, and consulting services. They assist with application modernization and cloud services adoption, transforming legacy systems and integrating cloud-native services to maintain a competitive and agile position.

The company offers Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) support using Terraform and CloudFormation to design scalable, repeatable environments. Their migration services include tailored strategies and expert execution with minimal disruption, followed by ongoing managed services for seamless transitions. This comprehensive approach helps customers manage their AWS environments securely, efficiently, and in compliance with industry standards.

Colocation Services

The company offers a comprehensive suite of colocation options to meet diverse customer needs, from shared colocation to private suites. Shared colocation environments provide cost-effective, multi-tenant infrastructure for smaller deployments, while cabinets offer fully secured spaces in enterprise-grade Tier III data centers. For customers requiring more specialized environments, Lightedge provides cage options that deliver private, dedicated space with tailored power, space, and access control requirements. The most comprehensive offering is the private suite, which provides complete control and isolation for sensitive workloads, featuring dedicated security protocols.

At the core of the colocation service is its technical support infrastructure, which operates around the clock with 24/7/365 remote hands support available from certified infrastructure engineers. This support extends to 24/7/265 coverage in certain cases, with the ability to provide geo-redundant support whenever needed. The data centers leverage N+1 and 2N power/cooling redundancy systems to maintain industry-leading service level agreements (SLAs), with carrier-neutral connectivity options available across all facilities.

Each facility combines high-density power capabilities with scalable space requirements and energy-efficient cooling systems designed to support modern IT infrastructure. The colocation service portfolio builds upon the company's existing capabilities in hybrid cloud connectivity, offering direct connections to major cloud providers including AWS and Azure. This hybrid readiness extends to private cloud options, with the ability to deliver geo-redundant support anywhere the company operates.

Acquisition of Connectria

Following the acquisition of Connectria, the combined company now operates 18 data centers across 12 U.S. markets, serving over 1,700 customers in highly regulated industries. The integration brings together Lightedge's enterprise-class colocation, connectivity, and enterprise cloud capabilities with Connectria's strengths in IBM and public cloud maturity, creating one of the few secure and compliant infrastructure providers in the country offering both hybrid colocation and tailored multi-cloud solutions.

The expanded service portfolio now includes a comprehensive suite of colocation options, from shared colocation environments for smaller deployments to private suites with complete control and isolation for sensitive workloads. Technical support infrastructure remains robust, with 24/7/365 remote hands support available from certified infrastructure engineers, extending to 24/7/265 coverage when needed. Data center facilities maintain industry-leading service level agreements through N+1 and 2N power/cooling redundancy systems, while offering carrier-neutral connectivity options across all facilities.

The acquisition has enhanced Lightedge's hybrid cloud capabilities, allowing direct connections to major cloud providers including AWS and Azure while maintaining strict compliance certifications and advanced security protocols. Environmental responsibility remains a core focus, with the company's sustainable infrastructure approach continuing to optimize energy efficiency while minimizing power usage, water consumption, and waste across all operations.
