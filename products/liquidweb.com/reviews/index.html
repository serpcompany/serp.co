From Basement to Global Leader: Liquid Web's 27-Year Journey in Data Center Technology

Since its founding in 1997 by Matthew Hill, Liquid Web has evolved from a single-basement operation to a multi-million-dollar global infrastructure provider. This evolution has spanned the entirety of the digital age, with the company consistently pushing boundaries in data center technology and hosting services. Today, Liquid Web manages over 500,000 customer accounts across seven continents, offering everything from small business WordPress hosting to enterprise-level private clouds. Their success story is one of adaptability in a rapidly changing industry, consistently delivering on their promise of 99.99% uptime across every tier of their service portfolio.

Company History and Foundation

The company's journey began in 1997 when Matthew Hill founded Liquid Web in his basement. From these humble beginnings, Liquid Web grew into a multi-million-dollar global enterprise, establishing itself as a dependable cornerstone for businesses and individuals since the late 1990s.

Data Center Infrastructure

Liquid Web's robust infrastructure spans multiple regions, with data centers located in Dallas, Washington D.C., San Francisco Bay Area, and major international hubs including London, Luxembourg, Hong Kong, Singapore, Lagos, and São Paulo. These facilities feature Tier-4 standards providing 99.99% uptime and implementing multiple redundant generators, advanced intrusion detection systems, and biometric access controls to protect customer data from cyber threats.

Hosting Services Portfolio

The company's comprehensive service offerings range from VPS hosting to dedicated servers, with all plans including 100% guaranteed power and network uptime through their Tier-4 facilities. Their three primary hosting solutions—Private Cloud Hosting, HIPAA Compliant Hosting, and PCI Compliant Hosting—offer scalable infrastructure for businesses of all sizes, from small websites to large-scale ecommerce operations.

Managed Hosting Tiers

Liquid Web offers multiple hosting support tiers to accommodate various levels of expertise and business needs. Their comprehensive support options range from self-managed basic support to premium tiers featuring 24/7/365 support, managed network and hardware, and proactive service monitoring. Enterprise customers benefit from dedicated solutions architects and customized configurations monitored via specialized solutions controllers for maximum uptime and performance.

Data Center Infrastructure

Located in multiple strategic regions—Dallas, Washington D.C., San Francisco Bay Area, London, Luxembourg, Hong Kong, Singapore, Lagos, and São Paulo—Liquid Web's data centers form the physical backbone of their global infrastructure. These facilities range from bare metal servers to sophisticated VPS environments, all operating within Tier-4 standards that guarantee 99.99% uptime.

The company's data center network employs an extensive array of security measures, including biometric access controls and advanced intrusion detection systems, backed by 24/7 Level 3 technician support and certified RedHat, Windows, and VMware expertise. Each center features multiple generators with N+1 redundancy, undergoes twice-yearly load-banking tests, and maintains redundant battery cabinets and Liebert Precision/Eaton Power distribution units to ensure continuous power supply.

Network infrastructure includes premium Tier-1 connections from Cogent, Level3, Verizon, and Comcast, supporting an impressive 8,500+ networks within a single network reach. Motion-detecting security cameras monitor facilities around the clock, while reinforced poured concrete walls protect against physical threats. The company's 27 years of experience managing 500,000+ customer accounts demonstrates their commitment to reliability and compliance across diverse market segments.

Hosting Services Portfolio

The company's service portfolio spans multiple tiers of virtual and dedicated hosting, tailored to address various business needs and technical requirements:

Server Hosting Options

The portfolio includes multiple specialized hosting solutions:

  • WordPress Hosting: Optimized with one-click installations, automatic updates, and enhanced security, starting at $21/month with a three-month promotional rate of 75% off ($5.25).
  • Cloud Hosting: Scalable with 10G network access, AMD EPYC processors, and dedicated bandwidth/storage, starting at $5/month with a three-month promotional rate of 50% off ($2.50).
  • VPS Hosting: Best for small businesses and developers, offering 10G network access, AMD EPYC processors, and Windows/Linux availability, starting at $5/month with a three-month promotional rate of 50% off ($2.50).
  • Dedicated Server Hosting: For high-traffic sites and resource-intensive applications, providing 99.999% uptime, SSD storage, and backup capabilities, starting at $44/month ($22/mo after three-month 50% off promotion).

In addition to these core offerings, the company provides specialized services including Magento Hosting, WooCommerce Hosting, and Private Cloud Hosting, each designed for specific business needs while maintaining their comprehensive 99.999% uptime guarantee.

Reseller Hosting

For businesses seeking to offer hosting services to their customers, Liquid Web provides multiple specialized programs:

  • The base Reseller Hosting program offers fully automated deployment and white-label solutions with 99.998% uptime, starting at $178/month ($113/mo after three-month 47% off promotion).
  • The IT Partner Program targets technology providers, allowing white-label services with custom branding and business associate support.
  • The Agency Partner Program, designed for digital agencies, offers recurring payouts up to 20% based on annual referral revenue.
  • The Refer-a-Friend Program rewards existing customers with $100 in hosting credits for each successful referral.

These programs support various business models, from traditional resellers to modern digital agencies seeking to expand their service offerings.

Additional Services

The portfolio includes multiple specialized services:

  • Email Hosting: Providing reliable service with large storage capacity and advanced features including shared calendars and mobile synchronization, starting at $2.25/month.
  • Magento Hosting: Specialized for large-scale ecommerce, featuring optimized performance and sophisticated features including Elasticsearch and free migrations, starting at $37.52/month ($22.76 after 44% off promotion).
  • Dedicated IP Addresses: Essential for businesses requiring unique web presence, included in most hosting plans and available as an add-on for additional flexibility.

Each service is supported by Liquid Web's comprehensive managed hosting tiers, offering varying levels of support from basic self- management to fully managed solutions with dedicated technical account management and proactive system monitoring.

Business Partnerships and Programs

Liquid Web's business partnership ecosystem consists of multiple specialized programs designed to support diverse business needs:

The IT Partner Program targets technology providers, offering white-label services with custom branding and business associate support. The Affiliate Program enables website owners and influencers to earn 300% or more of the sale value for each referral, with payment options including direct deposit, ACH, PayPal, or hosting credits. The Reseller Hosting Program allows existing hosting companies or technology providers to offer Liquid Web products with custom branding, delivering monthly payouts and priority support for clients.

The Agency Partner Program provides digital agencies with recurring payouts up to 20% based on annual referral revenue, while the Refer-a-Friend Program rewards existing customers with $100 in hosting credits for each successful referral. Each partner program spans multiple business models, from traditional resellers to modern digital agencies seeking to expand their hosting services.

Customer Support and Services

Managed hosting tiers provide varying degrees of support, from basic self-management to comprehensive professional services:

  • Self-managed: Basic support available, suitable for experienced users
  • Core managed: Includes chat with sales support
  • Managed: Offers 24/7/365 support, dynamic resource adjustment, and system monitoring
  • Premium: Features 24/7/365 support, managed network and hardware, reactive remediation for service failures, system-level health monitoring, and security updates

Enterprise accounts work with dedicated solutions architects who configure systems via specialized solutions controllers for optimal performance and uptime. The company's full-service management approach covers all aspects of server maintenance, from core operating system updates to comprehensive security monitoring.

Customer support operates through multiple channels, including phone, ticket, and live chat systems. Technical assistance is available globally via these channels, with dedicated account executives managing complex enterprise configurations. For agencies and resellers, the company offers comprehensive support programs including recurring payouts and performance-based incentives.

The company's support infrastructure handles over 500,000 customer accounts across diverse market segments, demonstrating their ability to scale and maintain service across multiple regions. Their 27 years of experience in hosting management contribute to their reputation for reliability and technical expertise.
